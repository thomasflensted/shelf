Shelf

A small, native ebook library for macOS. The parts of Calibre you actually use.

01

Grid or list

Browse your books as a cover grid or a sortable list.

02

Search

Find any book by title, author, series or tag.

03

Add books

Drop files or folders on the window, or press ⌘O. EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, PDF, CBZ and more.

04

Metadata from the file

Title, author, cover and ISBN are read straight from EPUB, MOBI, AZW3 and PDF files.

05

Book info

Press ⌘I for details, rating, description and subjects from Open Library and Google Books. No API keys needed.

06

Edit

Press ⌘E or double-click a book to change its title, author, description and cover.

07

Calibre import

Copy your Calibre library in, with metadata, covers and every format. The original is never touched.

08

Plain folders

Books live in ~/Books/Author/Title/ next to a single library.json. Pick another folder any time.

09

Send to Kindle

Plug in your Kindle and it shows up in the sidebar. Press ⌘K to send the selected books over USB.

10

On your Kindle

Green checks mark books that are already on the device. See storage use and every book with its size and source.

11

Kindle clean-up

Remove the leftover folders deleted books leave behind. Amazon books are never touched.

12

Copy to Shelf

Bring books that only live on your Kindle back into your library, then eject with ⌘E.

Install

Shelf needs macOS 14 or later. Kindle support works with Kindles that mount as a USB drive (10th-gen Paperwhite and older).

Download Shelf

1. Move it to Applications

On the download page, click the .zip file under Assets. Double-click it once it's downloaded, then drag Shelf into your Applications folder.

2. Open Shelf

macOS will say it can't check the app. Click Done, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.

3. Add your books

Drop a folder of books on the window, or choose File → Import Calibre Library to bring everything over from Calibre.
